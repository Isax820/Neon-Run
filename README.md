<h2>🎮 HOW TO PLAY</h2>

<p>
  Run, jump, and survive. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd> to jump.
  Press again mid-air to perform a double jump. Land on floating platforms,
  dodge obstacles, and collect coins to boost your score.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>⚡ FEATURES</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Infinite procedural level generation</li>
  <li>Progressively increasing speed as your score climbs</li>
  <li>Double jump — use it wisely</li>
  <li>3 types of obstacles: spikes, tall walls, and wide blocks</li>
  <li>Floating platforms to use as shortcuts or safe havens</li>
  <li>Coins to collect for bonus points</li>
  <li>3 lives with temporary invincibility after taking a hit</li>
  <li>Neon particle effects, parallax star field, and glowing visuals</li>
  <li>Session high score tracking</li>
</ul>
